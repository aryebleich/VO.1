Questions

Will I make money?

Nobody can promise that. Most active strategies underperform simply holding an index after costs. QuantPocket shows you how an idea behaved in the past, how it held up out of sample, and the range of outcomes to expect, so you can decide with your eyes open.

Who holds my money?

Your brokerage account is opened and held by Alpaca Securities LLC, a FINRA/SIPC member. QuantPocket connects to it with API keys you control, and you can revoke them at any time.

How does the performance fee work?

Each month we compare your account to its previous peak, adjusted for deposits and withdrawals. You only pay the plan's percentage on value above that peak. Losses must be recovered before any new fee is charged.

Is texting trades secure?

Commands only work from the phone number you verified, and every order needs your personal PIN. You can pause all bots and scheduled trades with one text.

What is QuantConnect?

QuantConnect is a professional algorithmic trading platform. Connect your QuantConnect account to create projects and run cloud backtests of Python LEAN algorithms right from QuantPocket.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Plans are month to month. You can also export or delete all your data from Settings.
