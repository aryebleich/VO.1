Trading involves risk

Investing in stocks and ETFs involves risk, including the possible loss of your entire investment. Automated and frequent trading can magnify losses and increase costs and taxes.

Hypothetical results

Backtests and projections are hypothetical. They are calculated with the benefit of hindsight, do not reflect actual trading, and may not account for slippage, liquidity, fees, taxes or market impact. Projected annual returns are statistical ranges built by resampling past returns. They are not forecasts or promises. Actual results will differ, possibly by a lot.

Automation risk

Bots, scheduled trades, SMS and voice commands can fail, run late, run more than once, or act on bad data. Speech recognition can mishear a command. Always monitor your account and keep the ability to act directly with your broker.

AI-generated strategies

The AI builder turns descriptions into rules automatically and can misunderstand you. Always review and backtest rules before using them.
